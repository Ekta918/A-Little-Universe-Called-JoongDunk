A note from the fans

On 19 October 2026, GMMTV announced that Joong and Dunk have mutually agreed to end their partnership as an acting pair. The statement says each decision was made with understanding and respect, with each artist's future goals in mind, and that previously announced work will go on as planned.

It hurts a little, and that's okay. It also means we got to watch something special grow. This page keeps those memories in one place, and we'll keep cheering for both of them, wherever they go next.

Our story

From a first look to the last announcement, here is the road we walked together.

  1. 2022

    Star in My Mind

    Their first main roles together. Dunk played Daonuea, and the series became a hit, with over 100 million views on YouTube and a Viu Award for Most Satisfying Series of the Year. The pair also came back for Sky in Your Heart and a special episode.

  2. 2023

    Our Skyy 2 and Hidden Agenda

    Fans got a two-episode reunion in April, then a brand-new series, Hidden Agenda. They also sang the theme songs for both together.

  3. 2024

    The Heart Killers

    A funny, action-filled romance, with Dunk as Style. The same year, both of them were announced as hosts of School Rangers.

  4. 2025

    Dare You to Death

    Another series, another song to sing together, with "Dare You to Love" arriving in 2026.

  5. 2026

    Eyes On You

    Their very own fan concert, plus appearances at GMMTV fan fests, including Japan. On 19 October, GMMTV announced the end of their partnership as an acting pair.
